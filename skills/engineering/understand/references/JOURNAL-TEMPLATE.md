## Quick Reference

### Mastery vs Reinforcement

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Concept Mastery Map | How much scaffolding to provide next time |
| Concepts to Reinforce | Worth a retrieval when it shows up again |

Do not copy every 🟡 Learning concept into Concepts to Reinforce.

### Mastery Levels → Scaffolding

| Level | Meaning | Scaffolding next time |
|-------|---------|------------------------|
| 🟢 Familiar | Can recognize and reason about it | Minimal |
| 🟡 Learning | Partial understanding or active questions | Some |
| 🔴 Explore | Needs guided explanation | Full |

### Pruning

Do this while updating the journal.

- Keep the latest 3–5 Session Log entries
- Remove a resolved Open Question once the resolution lives somewhere else, or it no longer matters for resumption
- Drop a Concept to Reinforce once it is 🟢 Familiar and another meeting would not help
- Merge or remove a mastery entry that no longer changes scaffolding
- Keep a genuine Aha Moment unless it repeats one already recorded
- Keep Focus & Goals and Current Mental Model current. No historical versions

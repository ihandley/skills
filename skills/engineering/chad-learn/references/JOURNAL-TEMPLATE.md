## Quick Reference

### Mastery vs Reinforcement

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Concept Mastery Map | How much scaffolding to provide next time |
| Concepts to Reinforce | Worth opportunistic retrieval when it naturally recurs |

Do not duplicate every 🟡 Learning concept into Concepts to Reinforce.

### Mastery Levels → Scaffolding

| Level | Meaning | Scaffolding next time |
|-------|---------|------------------------|
| 🟢 Familiar | Can recognize and reason about it | Minimal |
| 🟡 Learning | Partial understanding / active questions | Some |
| 🔴 Explore | Needs guided explanation or reconstruction | Full |

### Pruning (opportunistic, when updating)

- Keep the latest 3–5 Session Log entries
- Remove resolved Open Questions once the resolution is represented elsewhere
  or no longer matters for resumption
- Drop Concepts to Reinforce once 🟢 Familiar and deliberate retrieval no
  longer helps
- Merge or remove stale mastery entries that no longer affect scaffolding
- Preserve genuine Aha Moments unless redundant; don't casually discard them
- Keep Focus & Goals and Current Mental Model current—no historical versions

### How to Use This Journal

1. **Start** — Focus & Goals, Current Mental Model, latest `Current thread`,
   Open Questions
2. **Scaffold from mastery** — 🔴/🟡/🟢 decide how much guidance to give
3. **Update the mental model** only when understanding materially changes
4. **Capture ahas** only when the learner expresses them—preserve their wording
5. **Reinforce** only when a concept naturally recurs and retrieval helps
6. **Prune opportunistically** when writing the journal—not as a separate chore

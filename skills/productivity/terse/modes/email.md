# Email

- Use a specific subject when composing a complete email.
- State the purpose or decision in the opening paragraph.
- Put supporting context after the main point.
- Use bullets for multiple questions, requirements, or next steps.
- Stop after the context and action. Do not restate them.

## Platform features

When [../links.md](../links.md) says to link:

- Markdown: `[name](url)`
- HTML: an anchor whose visible text is that name
- Plain text: `name <url>`

Source: `Can you look at PROJ-123?`

Output: `Can you look at PROJ-123?`

Source: `The implementation is in the web-ui repo: https://github.com/example/web-ui.`
